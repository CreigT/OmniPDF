OmniPDF Privacy Policy

Operator: OmniPDF is operated and sponsored by CREIGNIFICENT LLC.

Effective: August 27, 2026

File processing

OmniPDF is designed to process supported PDF and image operations in your browser. Files processed through browser-based tools are not intentionally uploaded to OmniPDF servers for document processing.

Account and payment information

If you sign in or purchase a paid plan, information required to provide those services may be processed. Payments are handled by Stripe; OmniPDF does not store full payment-card numbers.

Local browser data

Some preferences, usage counters, and application history may be stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser storage may remove that information.

Third parties

Third-party infrastructure or payment providers may process limited technical or transaction information needed to provide their services under their own privacy terms.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to CREIGNIFICENT LLC through the contact information published with the service.
